# AlphaScope — Long/Short Equity Research Platform

**Student:** Tucker Martin

AlphaScope is a planned stock research platform designed to help people learn how investors research companies and think about portfolio decisions.

**Intended users:** College students, student investment clubs, beginner investors, and aspiring equity research analysts.

## Planned features

The following features are planned for future development and are not implemented yet:

- Stock price tracking and watchlists
- Long/short investment analysis
- Company valuation and sector comparisons
- Hedging and portfolio risk analysis
